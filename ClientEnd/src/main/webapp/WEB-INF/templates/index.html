Welcome

This is my small project, "Study_Recorder_Program". I use it to record the key concepts I learned throughout the school sessions, while I am pursuing my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Data Science at University of Wisconsin-Madison. If you have any question, I would be more than happy to hear it through my LinkedIn page.
It integrates modern backend technologies such as Spring framework, Spring MVC, and MyBatis. The data in this program, such as the school terms, the courses, and the notes, are dynamically displayed, meaning the pages are not literal or static. MySQL is used for most relational data storage, while key-value database Redis is utilized to store session ID and quota quantities.
Please note the course descriptions here are copied VERBATIM from the website of University of Wisconsin-Madison. I don't claim any of the copyright.
Again, I am still an undergrad and am still learning, so PLEASE SHOW MERCY if you're attacking my website. :)
