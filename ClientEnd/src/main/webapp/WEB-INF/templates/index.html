Welcome to My Study Recorder

Welcome to my digital brain dump, aka "Study_Recorder_Program"! This is where I attempt to make sense of the academic chaos while pursuing my Bachelor's in Computer Science and Data Science at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. If you're brave enough to peek into my educational journey, feel free to connect on LinkedIn - I promise I don't bite (most days).

This isn't just any ordinary project; it's a technological smorgasbord featuring the Spring framework, Spring MVC, and MyBatis. The contents here are fresh and dynamic! MySQL keeps my relational data in check, while Redis handles the behind-the-scenes magic of session IDs and quota quantities (reset daily). And because I like to keep things shipshape, MySQL, Tomcat, Nginx, and Redis are all neatly packaged in Docker containers - it's like a digital version of those Russian nesting dolls, but with less vodka and more code.

Lastly, a humble request from your friendly neighborhood undergrad: If you spot any quirks or hiccups on this site, remember - I'm still learning the ropes. So, before you unleash your inner critic, consider this my digital puppy-dog eyes pleading for mercy. Pretty please? :)
